FAQ

Common questions

Do I need an account to report?

Yes. Sign in or create a free account so we can keep you updated. Your phone and email are only visible to authorized staff.

How long does a fix take?

Each complaint has an inspection target of 48 hours. Progress is shown as it moves through each stage.

Who can see my details?

Only admin staff can see your name, email and phone number. Public pages show the issue, ward and status only.

See a problem right now?

It takes less than a minute to report it.
